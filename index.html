Research

Three interconnected research directions that shape my PhD goals.

NLP & Low-Resource Language AI

I work on language technologies for Bangla and linguistically diverse, low-resource settings, including regional language understanding, named entity recognition, hate-speech detection, healthcare language processing, and code generation from natural-language instructions. I am particularly interested in adapting language models to domain-specific and resource-constrained environments.

  • Large language models & domain adaptation
  • Bangla NLP, regional dialects & NER
  • Hate-speech detection & language understanding
  • Natural-language-to-code generation
  • Low-resource language modeling
Explore language research →

Computer Vision & Multimodal AI

I work on the intersection of language and visual understanding, developing and evaluating models and datasets for visual question answering, image classification, and vision–language learning. My work includes Bengali VQA, transformer-based image recognition, and multimodal applications in healthcare and other real-world domains.

  • Vision–language models & visual question answering
  • Bengali VQA & multimodal dataset development
  • Transformer-based image classification
  • Multimodal AI for healthcare
  • Computer vision for safety-related applications
Explore the datasets →

AI for Healthcare & Public Health

My healthcare research applies NLP, multimodal AI, and machine learning to health information and risk assessment, particularly in low-resource and LMIC contexts. My work includes AI-based risk assessment for sexual, reproductive, and mental health (SRMH), Bangla healthcare language processing, healthcare paraphrasing, and conversational AI for health information.

  • Healthcare LLMs & conversational AI
  • AI-based risk assessment for SRMH
  • Bangla healthcare NLP & paraphrasing
  • Multimodal AI for healthcare
  • AI for health information accessibility in low-resource settings
